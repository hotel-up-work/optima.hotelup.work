# Optima Collection Kamianets-Podilsky

Live site: https://optima.hotelup.work

## About
Optima Collection Kamianets-Podilsky is a modern city hotel with 38 rooms, located at vul. Starobulvarna, 2, Kamianets-Podilsky, 32301, close to the historic Old Town. It positions itself as a comfortable base for both tourist trips and business visits, combining accommodation with restaurant, conference, and relaxation facilities. Room categories include Standard, Superior, Junior Suite, and Suite, ranging from 17 to 63 square meters.

## Amenities
- Restaurant Primavera (Italian and Ukrainian cuisine, breakfast, business lunch, dinner, banquets, summer terrace)
- Two conference rooms (65 sq m "Kamianets-Podilsky" and 40 sq m "Serednovichnyi")
- Banquet hall for events
- Sauna
- Wi-Fi
- Parking
- Generator for backup power
- Designated shelter space during air raid alerts
